Lasagna

    Ingredients
  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion and garlic
  • Olive oil
  • Sliced mozzarella cheese
  • Sliced ham
  • Béchamel sauce
  • Salt, black pepper, and oregano to taste
    Guide to prepare
  1. Make the meat sauce: Sauté chopped onion and garlic in olive oil, brown the ground beef, add tomato sauce, season with salt, pepper, and oregano, then let it simmer for 15 minutes
  2. Make the Béchamel: Melt butter in a saucepan, stir in all-purpose flour, gradually whisk in milk over medium heat until thickened, and season with salt and nutmeg.
  3. Cook the noodles: Boil the pasta sheets according to package instructions until al dente, drain, and set aside (skip this if using oven-ready noodles).
  4. Layer the lasagna: In a baking dish, spread a thin layer of meat sauce, then add noodles, meat sauce, ham, Béchamel, and mozzarella; repeat until the dish is filled.
  5. Top and bake: Finish with a final layer of sauce, cover with plenty of mozzarella and grated Parmesan cheese, and bake at 180°C (350°F) for 25 to 30 minutes until golden and bubbling.
  6. Rest and serve: Let it rest for 10 to 15 minutes before slicing so the layers stay firm and intact.
