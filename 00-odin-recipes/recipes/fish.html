Fish

    Ingredients
  • White fish fillets
  • Olive oil
  • Minced garlic
  • Fresh lemon juice and zest
  • Butter
  • Cherry tomatoes
  • Fresh parsley and thyme
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
    Guide to prepare
  1. Preheat the oven: Preheat the oven to 200°C (400°F) and lightly coat a baking dish with olive oil.
  2. Season the fillets: Pat the fish fillets dry with paper towels and season both sides evenly with salt, black pepper, and minced garlic.
  3. Mix the marinade: Whisk together olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, and chopped herbs in a small bowl.
  4. Arrange in baking dish: Place the seasoned fillets in the dish, spoon the lemon-herb sauce on top, and scatter cherry tomatoes around them.
  5. Bake until tender: Dot each fillet with small pieces of butter and bake for 12 to 15 minutes until the fish is opaque and flakes easily.
  6. Garnish and serve: Drizzle with the pan juices, garnish with fresh parsley, and serve immediately with rice or roasted vegetables.
