Feijoada

    Ingredients
  • Black beans
  • Smoked pork ribs
  • Dried beef (carne seca)
  • Smoked sausage (paio and calabresa)
  • Bacon
  • Onion and garlic
  • Bay leaves
  • Olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
    Guide to prepare
  1. Prepare the meats: Desalt the dried beef and pork ribs in cold water overnight, changing the water periodically, then cut into bite-sized pieces.
  2. Cook the beans and meats: In a large heavy pot, add black beans, dried beef, pork ribs, and bay leaves; cover with water and simmer gently until the meats begin to soften.
  3. Add sausages and bacon: Sauté diced bacon and sliced sausages in a skillet until browned, then transfer them into the simmering bean pot.
  4. Season the stew: In the same skillet, sauté chopped onion and minced garlic in olive oil until golden, mash a ladle of beans into the mixture, and stir it back into the pot.
  5. Simmer to thicken: Continue cooking over low heat for 30 to 40 minutes until the broth is rich, savory, and thick, adjusting salt and pepper to taste.
  6. Rest and serve: Let the feijoada rest for 10 to 15 minutes before serving hot with white rice, farofa, sautéed collard greens, and orange slices.
